How it fits together
  • The Hub is small — a Next.js web UI, one Lambda (orchestrator), and two DynamoDB tables: workflows for state, events for traces.
  • The fleet is stateless — agents on Bedrock AgentCore Runtime are invoked on demand with a payload; they read and write through Jira.
  • Jira is the bus — every state change flows through tickets. Jira webhooks fire back into the Lambda to drive the next step.
